# Payment Reminder SaaS

A lightweight payment reminder application that helps small businesses automate payment follow-ups and improve collections.

## Problem

Small businesses often manage pending payments manually using:
- WhatsApp
- Excel sheets
- Notes
- Manual follow-ups

This leads to:
- missed reminders
- delayed payments
- poor cash flow
- repetitive manual work

## Solution

This application automates payment reminders and helps businesses track pending payments in a simple and efficient way.

---

# MVP Features

## Customer Management
- Add customers
- Store phone numbers and contact details
- View customer payment history

## Payment Tracking
- Create payment entries
- Set due dates
- Track pending and paid amounts

## Automated Reminders
Send reminders:
- Before due date
- On due date
- After overdue

## Reminder Templates
Predefined reminder styles:
- Friendly
- Professional
- Urgent

## Payment Status
Track statuses:
- Pending
- Paid
- Overdue

## Dashboard
Overview of:
- Total pending amount
- Overdue payments
- Upcoming dues
